# Shared player equipment adapter

C2.5 `EquippedTool` renders one held item and submits primary/alternate requests to one shared `AttackExecution`. `EquippedItemDefinition` references two independent attack profiles plus texture/grip/scale/rotation. Axe and sword share the adapter; only temporary starter selection references the two defaults. No per-weapon attack state machines or inventory.

Primary sweep starts from the existing held pose, eases into the snapshotted wind-up, and carries continuous angular velocity through the locked contact direction. Matching cubic tangents remove the former pause at impact; a subtle 3px outward arc gives the swing some weight. Alternate thrust smoothly aligns from the held angle, briefly draws back 2px, then accelerates into its 28/32px axe/sword extension. Maximum extension still matches the original contact sample. Both finish or interrupt into a 0.12s blend from the last displayed pose back to current aim, avoiding a forced end-pose snap. All movement here is cosmetic: damage, physical reach, attack duration/contact/cooldown and C3.2 reactions are unchanged.

The shared `AttackProfile.SweepWindupFraction` retains its existing Inspector control. `AxeConfig` supplies Enabled/HandAnchor/OrbitRadius/AimSpeed; starter attacks and item art/scale come from the equipped item Resources. `StarterAxe.tres` now scales the axe to 0.144 per axis (10% below 0.16); sword scale is unchanged. The old HitQuery child/API remains for compatibility callers/tests.

Keys 1/2 select axe/sword; 3 toggles a separate shield flag. Shield starts off. Switching rejects during actions or outstanding attack cooldown, so it cannot bypass recovery. RMB routes to guard only when equipped, otherwise to a visual hop. The optional blue guard arc is debug geometry, not shield artwork. Starter selection/flag is temporary, resets to axe/shield-off on scene load and is not inventory or persisted equipment.

Attack physics wakes only for accepted swings/cooldowns and sleeps on completion/cancellation. The pre-swing angle/orbit, wind-up fraction and thrust pixels are captured once when the attack is accepted; no new timer, Tween, Node or idle callback was introduced. The existing mouse-facing render callback remains and also positions the held item during a hop. Death, disabled scene ownership and exit cleanly cancel/unsubscribe/dispose.
