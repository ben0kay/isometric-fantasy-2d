# Factions

Factions describe **who an entity belongs to**, not what species it is or whether it is always hostile. Future examples: kingdoms, bandit bands, goblin clans, necromancers' armies, independent traders.

A Human can be a guard or a bandit; a Goblin may belong to a clan; multiple species can belong to the same kingdom. Faction relationships (friendly/neutral/hostile), alliances and reputation may change during play.

Eventually define faction data/resources with stable IDs and relationships. Do not hardcode one faction per race. **Status: placeholder only; no faction engine or faction assets implemented.**
